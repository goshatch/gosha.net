Five moments in Ramallah, West Bank, Palestinian Territories.

Written after two days spent there in the month of January, 2008; revised and edited April, 2009.

Further edits for publication on this website in September 2026:

  • Fixed some spelling and grammar mistakes;
  • Added alt tags to photographs;
  • Otherwise left the text unchanged, including parts I would write differently today.
